# Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

`travel-planner-latam` is a Claude skill: a set of written instructions. It contains no code that runs on your device or on a server, and it has no servers of its own.

## What this plugin collects

**Nothing.** The author of this plugin does not collect, receive, store or sell any data about you or your trips.

## How your data moves when you use it

When you use the skill, Claude follows its instructions with the tools that you have connected in your own Claude account:

- **Travel connectors** (Expedia, eDreams, Booking.com, Tripadvisor, Viator): Claude sends your search details (for example destination, dates, number of travelers and ages of children) to these services to get results. Each service handles that data under its own privacy policy.
- **Claude in Chrome** (optional): Claude types your search into travel websites in your own browser.
- **Google Calendar** (optional): Claude reads your calendar to find date conflicts. It does not create, change or delete events.
- **Claude**: your conversation is handled by Anthropic under the terms and privacy policy of your Claude account.

## What the skill never does

- It never asks for or stores passport numbers, payment card numbers or other identity documents.
- It never books, reserves or pays.
- It never signs in to a website or enters personal or payment data.
